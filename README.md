# Pressure-Proof Auction Board

An interactive dashboard of the most reliable players under pressure that Rajasthan Royals can bid for at the IPL 2027 auction. It was built for Round 1 of SupeRR Selector Season 2.

- `index.html`: the dashboard, a single self-contained page. It shows player cards, the top-10 boards, a squad builder and how the ratings work.
- `Pressure-Proof_Methodology_Report.pdf`: the full methodology report.

This is an independent entry. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rajasthan Royals, the IPL or Unstop.

Player photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are used under the licences credited on the page. A silhouette marks a player who has no freely licensed photo.
